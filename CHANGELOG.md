# Version 1.1

On a Kasner torus we compare scalar transport between Cauchy surfaces with axis-mode Hankel solutions. At an off-surface point the evaluation error against Hankel is $2.41\times10^{-8}$, and the covariance residual within the numerical transport is $3.08\times10^{-9}$. Localized frequency tests distinguish the two time branches, and pairings with smooth tests detect the translated singularities of Example 4.7. A final experiment compares three scalar presentations on overlapping Kasner slabs.

The proof of Lemma 11.1 now cites Paper 3, Definition 7.7 for re-marking the displays.

The computational supplement paragraph gives local and release links, and the disclosure identifies the original and additional computational environments.
